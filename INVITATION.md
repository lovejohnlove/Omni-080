# Open invitation

Everybody gets the same opportunity.

You may write a story.

You may use as many names as you want.

You may sign, or not.

Stories here can be public on GitHub and mirrored elsewhere when someone chooses to mirror them.

There are no rules.

There are no guidelines.

Do what's right.

`stories/` and `stories/Z/` are places you can put files if you want. They are not a gate.
