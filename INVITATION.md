# Open invitation — same opportunity for everyone

You may write a story.

You may choose your own **pen name**.

You may **sign** your story.

Your story may be pushed to this **public** repository and archived on **Google Drive**, **Dropbox**, and (when someone has bandwidth) **archive.org**.

## Rules (minimal, same for all)

1. **Own your pen name.** Do not impersonate a living person without clear consent. Fictional personas are fine.
2. **Sign the piece.** End with pen name and date (ISO or plain). Optional: one-line motto.
3. **Markdown preferred.** One story per file. Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD_pen-name_short-title.md` when possible.
4. **No technical ledgers here.** Hashes, manifests, and verification belong in private or technical repos.
5. **No requirement to be “good.”** Interesting is enough. Incomplete is allowed if labeled as a fragment.
6. **License.** Unless a file says otherwise, contributions are offered under the same terms as this repo’s LICENSE, so the commons can stay readable and remixable.

## Where files go

- `stories/` — main shelf  
- `stories/Z/` — second room (experiments, alternate takes, Z-labeled series)

## Archives beyond GitHub

| Place | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **GitHub (this repo)** | Public, versioned, linkable |
| **Google Drive** | Group working archive |
| **Dropbox** | Parallel durable copy |
| **archive.org** | Long-term public freeze of a release zip or folder |

Mirror cadence is human, not automated. A dated folder name (e.g. `Omni-080_stories_2026-11-07`) is enough.

## Closing

The opportunity is equal. The shelf is quiet. Write if you want the doing of the writing; not because a final chord is required.
