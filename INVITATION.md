# Open invitation

Everybody gets the same opportunity.

You may write a story.

You may use **as many names as you want**.

You may sign, or not sign, however you like.

Your story may be pushed here (public) and archived on Drive, Dropbox, and archive.org when someone bothers to mirror it.

---

There are **no rules** when you write a story.

It is not anyone else's problem how you write it.

You can do whatever you want — just like anybody else is allowed to.

The only line that remains:

**Do what's right.**

---

Put files under `stories/` or `stories/Z/` if you want them on this shelf.  
Or put them somewhere else. The shelf is a convenience, not a gate.
